## 🤖 About Community Poke

**Community Poke** is a [Poke](https://poke.com) instance that was turned into a Discord bot in the Poke Community Discord server.

> Nothing Community Poke does is endorsed by The Interaction Company of California or Cognition.

> **Note on the repositories:** mind the repos. Most of them were made by one dude. For the story, come to Discord and ask about Wolfy.

Come say hi at **[poke.com/discord](https://poke.com/discord)**.

For a plain-language overview of how the bot works, see [docs/how-it-works.md](https://github.com/CommunityPokeOrg/.github/blob/main/docs/how-it-works.md).

---

## 🧭 Who we are

**CommunityPokeOrg** is the workshop of the Poke Community. Where the [**CommunityPoke**](https://github.com/CommunityPoke) account is the identity of the shared Poke instance itself, this organization is where the community's *projects* live: tools, bridges, libraries, and the occasional game that exists purely because someone in the Discord said "wouldn't it be funny if."

Everything here is public, built in the open, and maintained with a healthy mix of engineering rigor and community chaos.

## 📦 Projects

| Project | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [**poke-hermes-agent-bridge**](https://github.com/CommunityPokeOrg/poke-hermes-agent-bridge) | Public agent-to-agent bridge allowing Poke to trigger and interface with the Nous Research Hermes agent |
| [**smart-email-tracker**](https://github.com/CommunityPokeOrg/smart-email-tracker) | Privacy-aware email open tracking with intelligent filtering for proxy prefetches, MPP, scanners, and sender self-opens |
| [**sheet-schema-editor**](https://github.com/CommunityPokeOrg/sheet-schema-editor) | TypeScript/React library and UI for uploading, editing, schema-mapping, and exporting CSV/XLS/XLSX tabular data |
| [**peat-bog-incremental**](https://github.com/CommunityPokeOrg/peat-bog-incremental) | An incremental clicker game about harvesting peat bog compute broth, cooling servers, and upgrading server racks |
| [**popcat-clone**](https://github.com/CommunityPokeOrg/popcat-clone) | A classic Popcat clicking meme game with animated mouth, popping audio, and click counter |

Browse all of them at **[github.com/orgs/CommunityPokeOrg/repositories](https://github.com/orgs/CommunityPokeOrg/repositories)**.
